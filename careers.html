This Is A Good Job For The Right Person

Exterior cleaning is honest, physical, outdoor work with a satisfying feature most jobs don't have: at the end of every single day, you can point at something and say "that looked terrible this morning, and I fixed it."

We're not looking for experience. Equipment and technique are teachable, and we train from zero. What we can't teach is showing up on time, being careful with someone else's property, and caring whether the job actually looks right.

If that sounds like you, send us a note using the form below. We read every one.

Open Roles

Who We're Hiring

Exterior Cleaning Technician

Full-time & seasonal · Paid training · No experience required

Run house washes, roof soft washes and surface cleaning alongside an experienced lead. You'll learn equipment operation, detergent mixing, ladder safety and how to protect landscaping properly.

  • Comfortable working outdoors and at heights
  • Able to lift 50 lbs and stay on your feet all day
  • Valid driver's license preferred
  • Reliable, punctual and respectful on customer property

Crew Lead

Full-time · Experience required

Run your own truck and crew. Own the job from the customer walkthrough through the final inspection, and help train newer technicians.

  • Prior pressure washing or exterior cleaning experience
  • Confident talking with customers and setting expectations
  • Clean driving record required
  • Soft wash experience a strong plus
