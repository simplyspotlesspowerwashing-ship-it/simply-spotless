Roof Soft Washing

Those Black Streaks Are Alive

The dark staining running down most roofs is Gloeocapsa magma, a hardy blue-green algae that feeds on the limestone filler in asphalt shingles. It spreads by airborne spores, which is why it tends to appear on one house and then the whole street, and why it always shows up worst on the shaded north-facing slope.

It is not a cosmetic problem only. The algae mat holds moisture against the shingle, accelerates granule loss, and creates a foothold for moss and lichen — whose root-like structures physically lift and separate shingles. Left alone, it shortens the life of the roof.

Cleaning it off with pressure would destroy the roof faster than the algae will. We use the soft wash method recommended by the asphalt shingle industry: a low-pressure application of a cleaning solution that kills the organism outright, then a gentle rinse. The stains lift, the spores die, and nothing ever touches your shingles harder than falling rain.

FAQ

Roof Cleaning Questions

Will this damage my shingles or void my warranty?

No. High-pressure washing a shingle roof absolutely can — it strips protective granules and many manufacturers will void a warranty over it. Soft washing is the method the shingle industry itself points to, because the cleaning is chemical rather than mechanical.

How long until the streaks come back?

Because we kill the organism rather than just rinsing the surface, results typically hold for several years. Heavy shade, overhanging trees and humid climates shorten that; open, sunny roofs go longer.

What about my plants and gutters?

Landscaping is pre-watered and continuously rinsed throughout, and we flush everything thoroughly at the end. Downspout discharge areas get particular attention since that's where runoff concentrates.

Do you clean metal, tile and cedar roofs?

Yes — each needs a different approach and a different solution strength. Tell us what you have when you request a quote and we'll confirm the right method for your specific roof.

Can you do the roof and the house at the same time?

That's the ideal way to do it, and it's how most of our customers book. Roof runoff lands on the siding, so cleaning the roof first and the house second gives the best result — and bundling saves you a trip charge.
