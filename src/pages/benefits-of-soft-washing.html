The Short Version

Pressure Removes Dirt. Soft Washing Removes What Causes It.

Most of what makes a house look dirty isn't dirt at all — it's alive. Algae, mildew, mold and bacteria colonize exterior surfaces and feed on them. Blasting that growth with high pressure knocks the visible layer off the surface, but it leaves the root structure and spores embedded in the pores of the siding. That's why a pressure-blasted house often looks great for a season and then looks dirty again fast.

Soft washing inverts the approach. Instead of using force, it applies a cleaning solution at low pressure, lets it dwell long enough to kill the organism outright, and then rinses everything away at roughly the pressure of a garden hose. The surface isn't just visually clean — it's biologically clean, so it takes far longer to regrow.

Neither method is "better" in the abstract — using the wrong one is the actual mistake. A driveway wants real pressure. Your siding and roof want soft washing. Matching the method to the surface is most of what separates a professional job from an expensive repair.

FAQ

Common Questions

Is soft washing strong enough to really clean?

Yes — and in most cases it cleans more thoroughly than pressure. The strength comes from chemistry rather than force, so it works into textures and seams that a pressure stream passes over. The visible difference on a heavily stained home is dramatic.

What's actually in the solution?

A surfactant-based cleaner formulated for exterior organic growth, diluted to the right strength for the specific surface. Concentration is adjusted per job — a delicate painted surface and a heavily-stained north-facing roof do not get the same mix.

Is it safe around kids and pets?

We ask that children and pets stay indoors while we're actively working, which is standard for any exterior cleaning. Once surfaces have been rinsed and dried, the area is fine to use normally.

Which of my surfaces need which method?

As a rule: anything you walk or park on gets pressure; anything on the vertical face of your home, plus the roof, gets soft washed. We'll confirm the plan surface by surface during your free walkthrough.
