Our Story

We Started Simply Spotless For A Simple Reason

Too many homeowners were getting one of two experiences: a company that showed up with too much pressure and too little care and left damage behind, or a company that never showed up at all after promising a quote.

We built Simply Spotless around fixing exactly that. We answer the phone. We show up in the window we gave you. We use the right method for each surface — soft washing where pressure would do harm, real pressure where it's genuinely needed. And we walk the finished job with you before we pack up, because the only opinion that decides whether a job is done is yours.

We're local and owner-operated, which is less a marketing line than a practical fact: the person who quotes your job is invested in how your house looks from the street, because we live around here too.
