How To Get Great Before & Afters

The comparison only lands when both photos line up. A few habits make all the difference:

  • Shoot from the exact same spot — mark where you stood
  • Same time of day, so the light and shadows match
  • Hold the phone level and horizontal, not tilted
  • Get the whole surface in frame, not just the worst patch
  • Take the "before" every time, even when you're in a hurry
  • Crop both images to the same dimensions before uploading
