FAQ

Junk Removal Questions

How is pricing calculated?

By volume — the amount of space your items occupy in the truck. We look at the load, quote you a firm price, and only start loading once you've agreed to it.

Do I need to move everything outside first?

No. That's the whole point of full-service. We'll carry it out of the basement, the attic, the back bedroom or wherever it's sitting.

What happens to my stuff?

Usable items are routed to donation where we can, recyclables to recycling, and only what's genuinely left over goes to the landfill.

Can you combine this with a pressure washing job?

Absolutely, and it's a great pairing — clear the deck or driveway of junk, then wash the surface underneath while it's finally empty. Mention both when you request your quote.
