Before You Book

Quick Answers

How fast will I hear back?

Most quotes go out within 24 hours, and calls or texts during business hours are usually answered same day. If we're up a ladder we'll get back to you as soon as we're down.

Is the quote really free?

Yes — free and no-obligation. For many jobs we can quote accurately from your description and a couple of photos; for larger or more complicated properties we'll come out and look, still free.

Do I need to be home for the service?

Not usually. We need access to an outdoor water spigot and to the areas being cleaned. Most customers do like to be there for the final walkthrough.

What if it rains?

Light rain generally doesn't affect results, but heavy weather is unsafe on ladders and roofs. We'll call you and reschedule to the next available slot — no charge, no hassle.

How do I prepare?

We've written it all out on the Prepare For Your Service page. It takes about ten minutes and helps us do a better job.
