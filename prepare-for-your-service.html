Here's What To Do Before We Arrive

Nothing on this list is complicated, and we'll handle anything you don't get to. But the more of it that's done when we pull up, the more of our time goes into cleaning rather than moving your patio furniture around.

🏠 Outside The House

  • Move vehicles out of the driveway and away from the work area
  • Move patio furniture, grills, planters and toys clear of the walls
  • Take down wind chimes, flags, hanging baskets and holiday decorations
  • Roll up or remove door mats and outdoor rugs
  • Secure or remove pet enclosures, bowls and bedding
  • Clear a path to your outdoor water spigot

🪟 Windows & Doors

  • Close and latch all windows — including basement and storm windows
  • Make sure all exterior doors are fully closed and latched
  • Close any pet doors
  • Let us know about any window or door with a known leak or bad seal
  • Point out damaged screens, loose siding or cracked panes ahead of time

🌿 Plants & Pets

  • Move potted plants a few feet away from the house
  • Cover any especially delicate or prize plantings — or just tell us
  • Plan to keep pets indoors while we're working
  • Let us know about any hidden irrigation heads or low-voltage lighting

⚡ Power & Access

  • Unplug and cover exterior electrical items where you can
  • Unlock gates, or leave us the code
  • Turn off exterior motion lights and cameras if you'd rather not record us
  • Tell us about a security system that reacts to movement outdoors

FAQ

Day-Of Questions

Do I need to be home?

Not required. We need access to an outdoor spigot and to the areas being cleaned. If you won't be there, just let us know how to get into any locked gates and how you'd like to handle the walkthrough.

Will you use my water?

Yes — an outdoor spigot with normal household pressure is all we need. It's a modest amount, and it's included in the price we quoted.

How long will it take to dry?

Siding is typically dry within a few hours in decent weather. Concrete may stay damp longer, especially in shade. Nothing needs to be kept off-limits once it's rinsed.

What if the weather turns?

We'll call you. Light rain is usually fine; anything unsafe for ladders or roof work gets rescheduled to the next available slot at no cost.

What if I'm not happy with something?

Tell us during the walkthrough and we'll fix it before we leave. If you spot something after we've gone, call — the Spotless Guarantee means we come back and re-wash it free.
